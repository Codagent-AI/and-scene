# create-and-scene verification checklist

Merged from the lead's L-1–L-63 and implementor's I-1–I-74 checklists. Original IDs are retained together where their behaviors overlap; C-* rows cover definition gaps found during crosscheck. Source identifies the originating checklist, or `crosscheck` for a gap in both.

This is a verification plan, not executed evidence. Inspection means reading the named implementation, template, or skill files and confirming the stated contract. Unit, component, type, and contract checks are fast; integration and end-to-end checks are restricted to INT-001, INT-002, E2E-001, and E2E-002 in `test-plan.md`. No check here has an agent operate the product as a user. Helper screenshots and warnings remain advisory artifacts, not proof of visual quality; rendered, settled-screenshot judgment of text fit and legibility is not claimed by any check in this list.

References below use `scene` = `specs/evolving-scene-presentations/spec.md`, `skill` = `specs/presentation-skill/spec.md`, and `verification` = `specs/presentation-verification/spec.md`. Lead rulings in the run's `acceptance-assumptions.md` resolve newcomer mounting versus animation timing, current-state tokens, geometry-check boundaries, and the other previously recorded ambiguities; they do not change the approved definition.
